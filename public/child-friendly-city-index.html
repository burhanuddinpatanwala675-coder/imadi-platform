      About the methodology — Scoring criteria are drawn from Sweden's child-friendly urban design model, which prioritises safe streets, accessible play space, and children's emotional comfort in city planning decisions. For ~60 widely-known areas, scores are illustrative estimates based on general published characteristics — not verified field audits. For any other area, describe it in your own words for a tailored analysis. Wellbeing commentary reflects general findings in child development and urban planning literature — it is illustrative, not a clinical or individualised assessment.
      About this prototype
      Based on Urban Governance class presentation by Maria Aziz — "Reimagining the City Through the Lens of Children's Needs." Methodology draws on the Sweden child-friendly urban design model.
      Scoring logic
      Score = Safety (30%) + Play access (25%) + Mobility (25%) + Emotional comfort (20%), derived from keyword analysis of the area description. A prototype for demonstration — not a validated planning tool.

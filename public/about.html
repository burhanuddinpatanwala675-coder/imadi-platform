Founder's vision

Why I built Imadi

I started Imadi after seeing the same problem across different businesses: teams still running on manual work, spreadsheets, repetitive processes, and communication that never quite joins up.

I believe technology should remove unnecessary work, not add complexity to people's jobs — and that AI is only worth using when it's practical: solving a real business problem, saving real time, reducing mistakes, and helping people make better decisions.

Too often, that kind of technology stays out of reach for anyone outside a handful of large companies with big technology budgets. I want it to be accessible to ordinary, ambitious businesses too.

That starts with understanding how a business actually works before building anything — then designing the technology around that reality, not the other way around.

Child-Friendly City Index

An AI prototype that reads a description of a neighbourhood and scores it against safety, play access, mobility, and emotional comfort — then suggests what Stockholm-style child-friendly design would change. Built from Maria Aziz's Urban Governance class presentation, "Reimagining the City Through the Lens of Children's Needs."

Try the prototype →
🏙️

Score a neighbourhood in seconds

Describe an area in plain language and get a 0–100 Child-Friendly City Index score, broken down by safety, play access, mobility, and emotional comfort.

🇸🇪

Benchmarked against the Sweden model

Scoring criteria are drawn from Sweden's child-friendly urban design model, which prioritises safe streets, accessible play space, and children's emotional comfort in planning decisions.

🌍

~60 real places, pre-scored

From Lyari and Gulshan-e-Iqbal in Karachi to Södermalm in Stockholm and boroughs across London — any other area gets a tailored, keyword-based analysis instead.

💡

Concrete, sourced recommendations

Each result surfaces specific gaps and interventions — traffic calming, School Streets pilots, pocket playgrounds — tagged to the design precedent they come from.

🎓

Grounded in real coursework

Built from Maria Aziz's Urban Governance class presentation on reimagining cities through the lens of children's needs.

⚠️

Honest about its own limits

The prototype says so itself: it's for demonstration, not a validated planning tool — scores for named areas are illustrative estimates, not verified field audits.

ALICO Business Suite

Custom software we built for Hamza Shabbir of ALICO Enterprises, a Karachi-based general order supply business. This one isn't an Imadi product — it's a full business management system built around their exact workflow, from quotations through to a running ledger.

🧾

Every document, one system

Quotations, delivery challans, sales tax invoices, commercial bills, and purchase orders — each with custom sequential numbering and print-ready output carrying the client's own branding, stamp, and signatures.

📡

Works offline, syncs when back online

A Firebase-backed progressive web app that keeps working without a connection and syncs once it's back — built for a team that moves between office and site.

🔐

Admin and viewer roles

Role-based access keeps day-to-day staff in a view-only mode while the owner keeps control, with a full audit log tracking every change made.

📦

Stock and ledger that stay in sync

Stock adjusts automatically as challans are delivered, invoices are paid, and purchase orders are received — feeding straight into a running ledger and reports.

🏦

Payments, expenses, and bank accounts

Every payment and expense is logged against the ledger and bank accounts, so the business always has an accurate financial picture.

🌐

Built for how the business runs

PKR by default with NTN and GST fields for local tax compliance, an interface in English and Roman Urdu, and JSON backup/restore so their data is never locked in.

Imadi Logistics ERP

Custom software built for Imadi Logistics and Fulfillment. A full logistics and financial ERP — accounting, invoicing, purchasing, banking, fleet, and AI-assisted tools — wired end to end to a real backend, not a demo with mock data.

📒

A real accounting core

A full chart of accounts and manual journal entries, with a live debit-equals-credit check before you can post, and voiding an entry creates a genuine reversing entry rather than just hiding it.

🧾

Invoicing and purchasing with real status lifecycles

Invoices post, cancel, duplicate, and take payments; purchase orders and bills move through submit → approve/reject → receive or pay — each step backed by the actual workflow, not just a status label.

🏦

Bank reconciliation, done properly

Import a bank statement, auto-match transactions, manually match or create the ones that don't line up, then reconcile with a live balance-difference check before it's marked complete.

🚚

Fleet and shipments, tracked end to end

Drivers with licence tracking, vehicles with maintenance and fuel logs and service-due warnings, and shipments with a full tracking-event timeline and proof of delivery.

🤖

AI-assisted back office

Search, business insights, a weekly cash-flow forecast, duplicate invoice/bill detection, expense categorization, and OCR document scanning — all calling a real AI service, nothing faked client-side.

🔐

Built with audit and safety in mind

Role-based permissions, full audit logs, and database backups with a safety-conscious restore flow that defaults to a scratch database rather than overwriting real data.

ReNaz — WhatsApp Waste Collection

Custom software built for ReNaz, a WhatsApp-first dry-waste collection platform for Karachi. Customers and collectors never leave WhatsApp — the only human-operated screen in the whole system is the admin dashboard.

💬

Runs entirely over WhatsApp

Customers request a pickup and collectors manage jobs entirely through WhatsApp conversations — no app to download, matching how people already communicate.

📍

Matches the nearest available collector

New pickups are offered automatically to the closest eligible collector by real distance, with automatic reassignment if they reject or don't respond in time.

📦

A full pickup lifecycle, tracked end to end

From request through category selection, optional photos, collection, weighing, and payment — every pickup moves through a defined status flow both sides can check.

🖥️

One dashboard for the whole operation

Admins manage customers, collectors and approvals, live pickup status, pricing, transaction history, and operational reports from a single console.

⚖️

Category-based pricing, PKR per kilogram

Each waste category — plastic, cardboard, paper, metal, and more — carries its own rate, with a full pricing history as rates change.

🔒

Hardened through two rounds of security review

Independent audits before release closed real gaps — authenticated endpoints, verified WhatsApp webhook signatures, rate-limited logins, and race-condition fixes.

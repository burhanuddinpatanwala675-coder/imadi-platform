Maflow

Business. Simplified. Maflow brings quotations, delivery challans, invoices, sales tax invoices, purchases, customers and suppliers, products, payments, expenses, and a running ledger into one workspace — so a growing business can stop juggling spreadsheets and disconnected tools.

🧾

One place for every document

Quotations, delivery challans, invoices, sales tax invoices, and commercial bills — created, tracked, and printed as professional documents with signature and stamp sections built in.

👥

Built for a team, not one person

Owners invite their team with role-based access — sales, accounts, or warehouse — so each person sees exactly what their job needs, nothing more.

📊

Ledger, reports, and an audit log

Payments and expenses stay in sync with a running ledger, with reports and an audit trail so owners always know where the business actually stands.

🏢

Your company, private and branded

Each company's data is fully isolated. Add your own logo, stamp, and signature, and the documents you generate look like yours — not ours.

🌐

Built for how businesses here work

Local tax fields like NTN and STRN, PKR by default, and an interface available in English, Roman Urdu, or Urdu.

💬

Real help, not a ticket queue

A direct WhatsApp line to support from inside the app, alongside a 14-day free trial with no card required to get started.
